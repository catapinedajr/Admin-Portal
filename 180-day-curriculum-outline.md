## Progression Summary:
- **Month 1**: Simple concepts, everyday language
- **Month 2**: Technical understanding, economic basics  
- **Month 3**: Economic theory, sound money principles
- **Month 4**: Advanced economics, institutional reality
- **Month 5**: Philosophy, societal implications
- **Month 6**: Complete maximalist worldview, future vision

This curriculum takes learners from complete Bitcoin beginners to sophisticated Bitcoin maximalists over 6 months of daily learning.
