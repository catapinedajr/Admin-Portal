Glossary of Terms for Bitcoin Education App
Bitcoin (BTC): Decentralized digital currency, operates on a peer-to-peer network.
Blockchain: Distributed ledger recording all Bitcoin transactions, immutable and transparent.
Wallet: Software or device storing private/public keys for sending/receiving Bitcoin.
Private Key: Secret code granting access to Bitcoin in a wallet.
Public Key: Address for receiving Bitcoin, derived from private key.
Address: Shortened public key (e.g., 1A1zP1eP5QGefi2DMPTfTL5SLmv7DivfNa).
Transaction: Transfer of Bitcoin, recorded on the blockchain.
Block: Group of transactions added to the blockchain.
Mining: Process of validating transactions, solving computational puzzles for rewards.
Hash: Cryptographic function turning data into fixed-length string.
Node: Computer running Bitcoin software, maintaining blockchain copy.
Halving: Event reducing mining reward by half, occurs ~every 4 years.
Satoshi: Smallest Bitcoin unit (0.00000001 BTC).
Fork: Change in blockchain protocol, creating new chain (hard/soft fork).
Decentralized: No central authority, governed by network consensus.
Consensus: Agreement among nodes on blockchain state (e.g., Proof of Work).
HODL: Slang for holding Bitcoin long-term, not selling.
KYC: Know Your Customer, regulatory ID verification.
Cold Storage: Offline wallet for secure Bitcoin storage.
Hot Wallet: Online wallet, convenient but less secure.
Fiat: Government-issued currency (e.g., USD, EUR).
Exchange: Platform for trading Bitcoin (e.g., Coinbase, Binance).
Gas Fees: Transaction fees on Bitcoin network (not Ethereum-specific here).
Whitepaper: Bitcoin’s foundational document by Satoshi Nakamoto (2008).
