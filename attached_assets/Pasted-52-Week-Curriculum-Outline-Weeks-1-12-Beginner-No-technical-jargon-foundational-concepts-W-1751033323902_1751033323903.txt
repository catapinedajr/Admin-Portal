52-Week Curriculum Outline

Weeks 1-12: Beginner (No technical jargon, foundational concepts)





Week 1: What Is Money Really? – Value, trade, monetary history



Week 2: Digital vs Physical – Transition to digital assets



Week 3: Bitcoin’s Birth Story – Satoshi, 2008 crisis context



Week 4: Your First Bitcoin Concepts – Addresses, keys, wallets



Week 5: Sending and Receiving – Bitcoin transactions explained



Week 6: The Security Mindset – Protecting Bitcoin, avoiding scams



Week 7: Why 21 Million Matters – Scarcity, inflation, supply economics



Week 8: Bitcoin vs Everything Else – Comparing to gold, stocks, fiat



Week 9: The Network Effect – Bitcoin’s strength from user growth



Week 10: Mining Demystified – Miners’ role and importance



Week 11: The Blockchain Explained – Ledger basics, non-technical



Week 12: Lightning Fast Payments – Second-layer solutions

Weeks 13-26: Early Intermediate (Basic technical concepts)





Week 13: Public vs Private Keys – Cryptography basics



Week 14: Transaction Fees – Why they exist, how they work



Week 15: Bitcoin Halvings – Supply reduction events



Week 16: Nodes and Consensus – How Bitcoin stays decentralized



Week 17: Wallets in Depth – Hot vs cold, hardware vs software



Week 18: Bitcoin’s Energy Use – Mining’s environmental impact



Week 19: Privacy Basics – Bitcoin’s pseudonymity explained



Week 20: Forks and Upgrades – Soft vs hard forks



Week 21: Stablecoins and Bitcoin – Complementary digital assets



Week 22: Bitcoin in Commerce – Merchant adoption, payments



Week 23: Regulatory Landscape – Governments and Bitcoin



Week 24: Peer-to-Peer Vision – Bitcoin’s philosophical roots



Week 25: Altcoins Explored – Bitcoin vs other cryptocurrencies



Week 26: Bitcoin’s Volatility – Price swings and market dynamics

Weeks 27-39: Intermediate (Moderate technical depth)





Week 27: Blockchain Mechanics – Blocks, hashes, confirmations



Week 28: Mining Pools – How miners collaborate



Week 29: Layer 2 Scaling – Lightning Network in depth



Week 30: Smart Contracts on Bitcoin – RSK, Taproot basics



Week 31: Bitcoin’s Security Model – Game theory, incentives



Week 32: Decentralized Governance – Who controls Bitcoin?



Week 33: Privacy Tools – Mixers, CoinJoin, challenges



Week 34: Bitcoin in Developing Nations – Financial inclusion



Week 35: Market Analysis – Trading, cycles, sentiment



Week 36: Bitcoin and Gold – Digital vs physical scarcity



Week 37: Institutional Adoption – ETFs, corporate treasuries



Week 38: Cybersecurity Threats – Hacks, 51% attacks



Week 39: Bitcoin’s Cultural Impact – Art, memes, community

Weeks 40-52: Advanced (Technical and economic depth)





Week 40: Cryptographic Foundations – Hash functions, signatures



Week 41: Bitcoin’s Monetary Policy – Code as law



Week 42: Advanced Lightning – Channels, routing, scaling



Week 43: Taproot and Schnorr – Privacy, efficiency upgrades



Week 44: Bitcoin in DeFi – Decentralized finance integration



Week 45: Global Economic Impact – Bitcoin vs central banks



Week 46: Mining Economics – Hardware, energy, profitability



Week 47: Quantum Computing Risks – Future-proofing Bitcoin



Week 48: Bitcoin as a Protocol – Beyond currency



Week 49: Geopolitical Implications – Sovereignty, sanctions



Week 50: Hyperbitcoinization – Could Bitcoin replace fiat?



Week 51: Building on Bitcoin – Developer ecosystem



Week 52: The Bitcoin Expert – Synthesizing knowledge, future vision
