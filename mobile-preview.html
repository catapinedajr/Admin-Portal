💡 Why think in sats?

Satoshis (sats) are the smallest unit of Bitcoin. There are 100,000,000 sats in 1 Bitcoin. Thinking in sats helps you understand Bitcoin's true divisibility.

Example: 100,000 sats = 0.001 BTC = $117.04
