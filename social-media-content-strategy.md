# HODLearn Pre-Launch Social Media Content Strategy

## Overview
Transform your 30-day Bitcoin curriculum into engaging social content that builds audience trust and anticipation for your app launch. This strategy uses your "Conviction Through Curiosity" approach to create daily educational content across platforms.

## Content Pillars (Based on Your App's Framework)

### Pillar 1: "Your Money Matters" (Financial Urgency)
**Purpose**: Build immediate relevance around inflation and purchasing power
**Frequency**: 3x per week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday)

### Pillar 2: "Bitcoin Basics Made Simple" (9th Grade Education)
**Purpose**: Demystify Bitcoin using everyday language
**Frequency**: 2x per week (Tuesday, Thursday)

### Pillar 3: "Freedom Friday" (Financial Sovereignty)
**Purpose**: Inspire with Bitcoin's empowerment potential
**Frequency**: 1x per week (Friday evening)

### Pillar 4: "Community Wisdom" (Social Proof)
**Purpose**: Share success stories and build community
**Frequency**: 1x per week (Saturday/Sunday)

## Platform-Specific Adaptations

### X (Twitter/X) - Primary Platform
**Format**: Text threads + images
**Style**: Conversational, data-driven
**Example Thread**:
```
🧵 Your grocery bill just exposed the biggest lie about money

1/ You spent $130 this week on the same cart that cost $100 last year

2/ That's not "inflation" - that's your money being quietly stolen

3/ Here's how... [continue thread]
```

### YouTube - Educational Hub
**Format**: 60-90 second educational videos
**Style**: Visual storytelling with simple animations
**Series Ideas**:
- "Bitcoin in 60 Seconds"
- "Money Myths Busted"
- "Your Financial Freedom Journey"

### Instagram - Visual Storytelling
**Format**: Carousel posts + Reels
**Style**: Clean graphics, bite-sized education
**Content Types**:
- Before/after purchasing power graphics
- Step-by-step Bitcoin explanations
- Quote cards with statistics

### TikTok - Viral Education
**Format**: Short-form educational videos
**Style**: Quick, engaging, trending audio
**Content Ideas**:
- "POV: You discover what inflation really is"
- "Things that cost more than Bitcoin mining"
- "Explaining Bitcoin to my grandma"

## Content Templates

### "Money Reality Check" Template
**Hook**: "Your money is worth less than you think"
**Problem**: Specific example (gas prices, groceries, rent)
**Solution**: Bitcoin's fixed supply
**CTA**: "Follow for daily money truth"

### "Bitcoin Simplified" Template
**Hook**: "Bitcoin explained like you're 5"
**Analogy**: Compare to familiar concept
**Key Point**: One main takeaway
**CTA**: "Questions? Drop them below"

### "Freedom Story" Template
**Hook**: Real person/country/company story
**Journey**: Their Bitcoin adoption path
**Result**: Freedom/sovereignty gained
**CTA**: "Your financial freedom starts here"

## Automation Strategy (N8N Workflow)

### Phase 1: Manual Testing (Weeks 1-4)
- Post manually to test engagement
- Track which content performs best
- Refine messaging based on comments/shares

### Phase 2: Selective Automation (Weeks 5-8)
- Automate winning content formats
- Maintain manual posting for timely/trending content
- Set up cross-platform scheduling

### Phase 3: Full Automation (Weeks 9+)
- Automated daily educational content
- Manual community engagement and responses
- Weekly trend-based content

## Engagement Strategy

### Daily Activities (30 minutes/day)
- Respond to comments with educational value
- Share relevant Bitcoin news with your perspective
- Engage with Bitcoin education community

### Weekly Activities (2 hours/week)
- Analyze content performance
- Plan next week's trending topics
- Create community polls/questions
