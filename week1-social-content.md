## Strategy: Build Curiosity Without Spoiling App Content

Your app teaches the "why" - your social content creates the "aha moments" that make people want to learn more. Think of social as the trailer, app as the full movie.

## Content Themes vs App Differentiation

### Social Content Focus:
- **Awareness**: Opening eyes to monetary problems
- **Curiosity**: Creating "wait, what?" moments  
- **Social Proof**: Real-world examples and stories
- **Community**: Building discussions and questions

### App Content Focus (Different):
- **Education**: Step-by-step learning progression
- **Understanding**: Deep dives into how Bitcoin works
- **Practice**: Safe simulation environments
- **Mastery**: Quiz-based knowledge verification

## Engagement Strategy

### Daily Interaction (15 minutes):
- Respond to comments with questions back: "What surprised you most?"
- Share surprising stats in replies
- Repost user comments that show lightbulb moments

### Content Variations:
- **Morning Posts**: Awareness/shocking facts
- **Evening Posts**: Community discussion starters
- **Weekend Posts**: Reflection and next week preview

## Measuring Success

### Week 1 Goals:
- 50+ thoughtful comments per post
- 10+ people asking "where can I learn more?"
- 5+ shares of your content with personal stories
- Building list of people interested in "learning Bitcoin properly"

### Content That Works = More Content Like It
- Track which posts get most genuine questions
- Double down on formats that create discussion
- Note which topics make people say "I never thought of it that way"

This approach makes your social content the appetizer that creates hunger for the full educational meal your app provides.
