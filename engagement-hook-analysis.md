## Modern Attention Span Psychology

### The 5-Second Rule
Users make decisions in 5 seconds based on:
1. **Immediate personal threat** (0-2 seconds)
2. **Clear benefit promise** (2-3 seconds)  
3. **Social proof/authority** (3-4 seconds)
4. **Simple next action** (4-5 seconds)

### Winning Hook Formulas

**Formula 1: Shock + Loss + Solution**
- "Your $50,000 salary buys what $35,000 bought 5 years ago"
- "Bitcoin holders avoided this theft"

**Formula 2: Specific Fear + Timeline + Action**
- "Your savings lose $137 every month to inflation"
- "Here's how to stop it"

**Formula 3: Social Proof + Scarcity + Urgency**
- "Smart money is quietly moving to Bitcoin"
- "While everyone else loses 8% annually"
