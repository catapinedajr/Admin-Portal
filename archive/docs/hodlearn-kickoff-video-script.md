# HODLearn™ Kickoff Video Script
## Target: 60-90 second promotional video for Twitter/social media

---

### OPENING HOOK (0-5 seconds)
**Visual**: Split screen showing coffee prices: "$2 in 2020" vs "$6 in 2024"
**Voiceover**: "Your money is disappearing... and you don't even know it."

### PROBLEM SETUP (5-15 seconds)
**Visual**: Fast montage of rising prices - groceries, gas, rent
**Text overlay**: "Everything costs more"
**Voiceover**: "While you're working harder, your paycheck buys less every single day. The system is rigged against you."

### THE SOLUTION REVEAL (15-25 seconds)
**Visual**: Bitcoin logo appears with orange glow
**Text overlay**: "There's a better way"
**Voiceover**: "Smart people are protecting their money with Bitcoin. But understanding it? That takes time."

### PRODUCT INTRODUCTION (25-45 seconds)
**Visual**: iPhone showing HODLearn™ app interface
**Text overlay**: "HODLearn™ - 5 minutes daily"
**Voiceover**: "Introducing HODLearn™. Just 5 minutes a day. Real Bitcoin education for busy professionals. No overwhelming theory. No confusing jargon. Just clear, practical knowledge that builds your conviction."

### CREDIBILITY & FEATURES (45-65 seconds)
**Visual**: App screens showing daily lessons, quizzes, wallet simulator
**Text overlays**: 
- "180 days of content"
- "Daily habit building"
- "Safe practice tools"
- "Mobile-first design"
**Voiceover**: "Six months of structured learning. Daily lessons that fit your schedule. Practice tools that keep you safe. All designed for people like you who want to understand Bitcoin without the overwhelm."

### CALL TO ACTION (65-75 seconds)
**Visual**: HODLearn™ logo with website/app store buttons
**Text overlay**: "Start free today"
**Voiceover**: "Your financial future is too important to leave to chance. Start building your Bitcoin conviction today."

### CLOSING (75-80 seconds)
**Visual**: HODLearn™ logo with tagline
**Text overlay**: "HODLearn™ - Understanding Bitcoin takes time. Building conviction takes community."
**Voiceover**: "HODLearn™. Because your money deserves better."

---

## VISUAL STYLE NOTES:
- Clean, professional aesthetic matching the app's dark theme
- Orange accent color (#f97316) for Bitcoin/brand elements
- Fast-paced but not overwhelming
- Mobile-first visuals showing actual app interface
- Real price comparisons, not stock footage

## TONE:
- Urgent but not alarmist
- Professional but approachable
- Confident and reassuring
- Direct and action-oriented

## KEY MESSAGING:
1. **Problem**: Inflation is stealing your money
2. **Solution**: Bitcoin protects against this
3. **Barrier**: Bitcoin is complex to understand
4. **HODLearn™**: Makes Bitcoin simple and accessible
5. **Action**: Start learning today in just 5 minutes

## MUSIC SUGGESTIONS:
- Subtle, building tension in problem section
- Hopeful, uplifting during solution reveal
- Confident, energetic during product demo
- Inspiring, forward-looking during call to action

---

**Total Runtime**: 75-80 seconds
**Target Audience**: Professionals earning $40K-$80K who feel financial pressure
**Primary Goal**: Drive app downloads and email signups
**Secondary Goal**: Establish HODLearn™ as the trusted Bitcoin education platform
